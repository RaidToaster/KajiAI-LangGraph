## What still requires semantic judgment

The contextual rule recognizes an immediately preceding colon-ended paragraph and a blockquote body only when their full literal span fits 250 words. It copies the source's introduction; it does not prove that its date, speaker attribution, authenticity, or completeness is correct. Other formatting and over-limit pairs are not automatically bound. A finding may state only what its chosen intact passage establishes, and unknown roles remain unknown. All original records means the current ranked-twelve-source, complete-paragraph, 3,500-character exposure, not every byte originally retrieved. No primary record was acquired here.

The registry still permits the model to omit an issue, wrongly label a real uncertainty nonblocking, or write an inaccurate issue reason. A single registry reduces inconsistent slots; it does not certify the classification. Different IDs can also describe the same issue. The compiler does not detect semantic equivalence, but any material version still blocks. Audit the entire registry, its reasons, neutral selector questions, source/action roles and component-to-finding links, including in abstentions. A source-relative note keeps its source/ID in the raw registry even though the legacy rendered note displays only component, question and reason. Read that raw registry during audit.

Removing component prose does not prove whole-claim support from a collection of locally faithful findings. Verify that the linked findings actually justify each exact component and C0, with the same target, time, quantity, negation and qualifications. Missing evidence is not refutation. Coherent conflicting evidence does not require settling the underlying value, but uncertainty about alignment or reliability can still block acceptance. Neither High nor `sources_independent=true` substitutes for this audit.

The unchanged deterministic gate retains gaps/contradictions and requires High, retrieved exact quotations, two hosts, resolved/cited assertions, actual counterevidence and source independence, plus both stances for CONFLICTING. The existing Jev citation check uses up to 1,200 surrounding characters as well as the quote. Consequently, Jev can support a finding using text outside its selected passage, as the retained prior failures already demonstrate. Jev >= .80 remains necessary, and selected-passage fidelity must be audited separately. Conditional provenance review remains necessary for would-be acceptance; an uncalled provenance check is not a pass. No new checker or materiality service is justified by this contract change.
