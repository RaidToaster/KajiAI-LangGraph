# Flash workflow comparison

## Question and candidate

Does presenting the analyzer with canonical evidence text and asking it to ground each finding in a contiguous passage improve label accuracy and the number of correct supported acceptances? The current full workflow is the comparator within each exact model. Both arms use the same graph, domain policies, citation review, resource settings and four-class output schema. Both keep query-exhaustion off. The candidate changes the analysis prompt and the representation of records in that prompt. The gate still requires literal quotes and the original support checks.

## Samples and paired runs

Use eight stratified AVeriTeC training cases for development and twelve separate training cases for confirmation. Draw without replacement from the frozen viewer slice at offset 1200 and exclude normalized exact claim-text overlaps with the final split. Use two and three claims per class respectively, with seed 260311 and a fixed interleaved model and arm order. Only claim text and date enter inference. Preserve complete trajectories and transport responses. Treat retrieval and cloud-model drift as residual variability.

The final case set is all 500 claims in the saved AVeriTeC development split in original row order. It was previously used for the Gemma study and for aggregate diagnostic evidence. It is a comparison on this split. The final comparison calls for 500 attempts per arm per model, 2,000 total. Confirmation must pass before final workflow/settings are released and frozen. Final records will use the identical manifest in both models and both arms.

## Scoring and decision

Execution failures are abstentions in failure-inclusive four-class accuracy and macro F1. The denominator includes every started case; untouched cells are tracked separately. Report paired changes, gate coverage, correct acceptances per attempt, incorrect acceptances per attempt, and label accuracy among accepted cases. Independently reconstruct quote provenance against the round's versioned text and match each accepted citation to its Jev judgment. Inspect newly accepted cases for stance, direct support for the finding and verdict, date, qualifications, and shared lineage. Track exact and Jev-supported citation counts and proportions across final assessments as diagnostic measurements. Separate automated/agent assessments from independent human annotations.

Development advances to confirmation only if each model gains at least one net correct, evidence-supported acceptance out of eight, adds zero incorrect acceptances, has no loss in raw correct count, and loses at most 0.02 macro F1. Every newly accepted candidate verdict must be supported by the audit. All candidate acceptances must have 100% exact and Jev-supported citations. Candidate execution failures cannot exceed baseline. Use the same requirements on the fresh twelve-case confirmation, with at least one net supported acceptance per model. A revision requires a documented development failure and justified mechanism. Reuse an earlier baseline only on the identical cells and settings and disclose time separation. A failed confirmation closes the frozen candidate. Any future candidate needs a new untouched confirmation sample. A pass is a screening result on a stratified sample.

For final reporting, use paired, claim-indexed bootstrap intervals with 10,000 resamples (seed 260311) for accuracy, macro F1, correct acceptance per attempt and incorrect acceptance per attempt. Compare workflow within each model; compare models separately. Quantify citation review, quality, service and execution outcomes even if quality criteria fail.

## Operations

Use existing Ollama, TinyFish Search and Fetch, and TypeSafe credentials. Ollama exact requested tags are `glm-5.3-flash:cloud` and `deepseek-v4.1-flash:cloud`. Record and check every returned ID, allowing only the requested cloud tag or its service-reported suffix-free ID. Keep model version `jev-1.13.0`. No paid overages or billing changes. Initial live development batch is one claim in both arms and models, up to 32 Ollama, 160 Jev, 24 Search and 48 Fetch sends. Continue in inspected batches of at most two cases, up to the frozen eight-case development phase. Graph limits are three rounds, two queries per round, two fetches per query, and a 270-second soft deadline. The worker has a 300-second wall limit and two numerical threads, and one worker runs at a time. Both arms request 4,096 output tokens, permit one existing schema repair per malformed output, and disable transport and search retries. Stop on quota, authentication, unexpected model identity, usage mismatch, unknown transport outcome or unsafe execution. Audit each completed batch. Never repeat a started case-arm-model attempt under the same phase.

Before final release, extrapolate observed per-model costs with reserve and reconcile the included balance, free retrieval limits and actual sends. TinyFish documents 30 Search/minute and 500/hour, and 150 Fetch URLs/minute and 1,000/day. Daily Fetch can span multiple reset windows. Track uncached list-price estimates separately from billed amounts. The small phases use separate scheduling stops of $3 Ollama and $0.30 Jev, retaining actual replies.
