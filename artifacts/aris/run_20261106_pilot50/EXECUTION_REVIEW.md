Verdict: operational fixes required before first live pilot call. This does not request or authorize a qualification/development loop.

1. **P1 — UNKNOWN retrieval outcomes do not block continuation.** `pilot50.usage()` marks every TinyFish send usage-known, regardless of missing terminal response. `reconcile()` checks unresolved outcomes only for Ollama/Jev. `launch()` ignores retrieval `incomplete` and timeout by itself. A worker killed during retrieval can therefore leave an unknown send and launch another attempt. Keep every service outcome UNKNOWN until terminally reconciled; stop before further dispatch, including after interruption/resume. Preserve its quota reservation.
2. **P1 — requested-output reservation is not conservative against the known cloud overrun behavior.** `PilotLedger.before()` reserves only the requested `num_predict` output; frozen `runtime_pilot.Ledger.response()` explicitly recognizes that cloud may ignore that setting and stops only after observing the overrun. The current send can therefore exceed its reservation and scheduling ceiling before the stop. Reserve a documented provider-enforced maximum completion liability (including reasoning where billed), or establish another defensible conservative bound before launch. Verify the rates backing the reservation; keep these scheduling limits distinguished from provider-enforced caps.
3. **P1 — preflight eligibility is unconditional.** `preflight()` writes `first_attempt_can_launch=True` even if derived headroom is negative; `launch()` only checks PREFLIGHT existence. Bind launch to a current, matching protocol/accounting/rate check, reject insufficient recorded headroom, and preserve the explicit distinction between dated reported balances and fresh account verification. This review did not independently verify any account balance or current price.
4. **P2 — unknown cost is rendered as zero in the report.** `analyze_pilot50` sums absent `estimated_usd` with a zero default. Unknown model sends retain reservations in the service ledger, but attempt/report totals show zero for those sends. Show known rate-derived cost, unknown-count/reserved liability, and conservative total separately; do not label the partial known total a reconciled full cost.

Additional robustness issue: frozen `flash_study.scan()` indexes `response['status']` before its guarded parse. An error-only response metadata file can raise KeyError, preventing terminal job/accounting export. A minimal accounting-layer normalization should preserve error-only/missing responses as UNKNOWN and allow durable STOP/report generation.
