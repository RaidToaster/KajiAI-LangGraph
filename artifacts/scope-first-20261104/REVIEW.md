## Exact requirements for a defensible new mechanism

1. **Keep the obligation set client-bound.** C0 remains the exact original claim and the other required assertion strings retain their qualifiers. A model-written scope description explains these strings; it cannot replace, narrow, omit or add to them. Planning statements/questions remain unverified leads. A speech-only obligation concerns the utterance; an embedded fact or causal obligation still needs its own substantive evidence. Do not change `questions_as_guidance` to a different obligation-generating mode silently.

2. **Make target relations explicit before conclusions.** A concise row per required component should identify the exact assertion, what is being adjudicated, linked observations, and how the claimed uncertainties could change the proposed evidentiary verdict. Provenance uncertainty can undermine a verdict even when it does not change a numerical threshold. For a CONFLICTING proposal, assess whether the opposing records genuinely concern the same target and carry valid evidentiary paths; adjudication does not require first deciding which side is true. For a threshold proposal, numerical disagreement can leave the same inequality intact, but conversion assumptions, dates, populations and evidence quality can still matter.

3. **Preserve materiality as a model judgment subject to audit.** New nonblocking limitations must be emitted as such in the new raw response, with a reason tied to C0/its component. They are not old gaps moved after generation. An unexplained or missing relation does not auto-clear anything. A limitation saying “independent origin unestablished” cannot coexist with an unexplained independence=true and pass merely because it sits in the nonblocking field. A new classification that hides a genuinely verdict-undermining uncertainty fails readiness even if the gate would otherwise accept.

4. **Keep the conservative union intact.** Every raw top-level gap and contradiction, every component material question, every origin material question including uncited/adverse origins, and any new scope-ledger material uncertainty enters the existing blocking gaps.
