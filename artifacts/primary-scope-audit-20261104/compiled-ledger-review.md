## Verified findings

1. **The central compilation behavior is correct and conservative.** `llm.py:440` binds C0 to the exact original claim through the client component dictionary. `indexed_assessment.py:56` copies explicit resolved booleans and confidence; it does not infer adjudication from IDs. Missing/unknown components, unknown finding links, duplicate finding/component IDs and invalid citations cannot silently supply required coverage. All returned findings become evidence items. Component questions declared material are unioned into gaps. A resolved component with a material question remains blocked. This addresses the observed copy-contract failure without declaring the underlying evidence sufficient.

2. **A concrete ledger-retention defect was found and fixed during this review.** The original compiler saved component references to arbitrary finding IDs while compiled `EvidenceItem` discarded `IndexedFinding.id`, `.source` and `.passage`. Saved origin rows also used source/passage IDs without their lookup. A standalone saved research-round assessment therefore could not resolve its ledger. The executor added `raw_indexed_assessment` and `indexed_input` storage fields: the former preserves the validated finding IDs/links; the latter preserves exact components and source/passages, plus planning leads, executed queries, date, domain and prior gaps from the live analyzer. I inspected the updated compiler, analyzer, model fields and JSON-roundtrip regression. The code now makes these links self-resolving without changing their gate semantics. This was an audit/reproducibility defect, not demonstrated unsupported acceptance. I did not run the regression; complete that check and ensure the diagnostic archives the raw transport/prompt too. No digest framework is needed.

3. **The adverse-origin concern was fixed during this review.** I initially read a version that unioned provenance questions only when their source was cited. The executor then changed the origin loop to union every explicitly declared material question, including an uncited adverse source, and added `test_uncited_origin_cannot_hide_a_declared_material_question`. I inspected the current source and test. The change is conservative and addresses omission of that negative field. I did not execute the test. The originally stated narrower cited-source contract should not be described as the current implementation.

4. **An origin ledger is still unverified metadata.** Empty/unknown origin/action fields or an empty passage list are schema-valid; the compiler checks referenced IDs, not entailment or independence. This is consistent with its declared role, provided no report treats a populated ledger as a passed provenance check. `graph.py:588` retains the separate conditional Jev provenance review after structural/citation eligibility. Freeze `require_independent_provenance_review=true` explicitly for the study: the general default configuration does not turn that existing opt-in review on. Retain citation confidence 0.80 and the original source/quote/assertion checks. I found no evidence that the new compiler bypasses them.

5. **Passage-level auditing remains essential.** `jev.py:143` judges surrounding context including the quote; `graph.py:551` supplies up to 1,200 surrounding characters each way. A Jev support judgment therefore does not establish that the selected passage alone entails the complete finding. This is an existing limitation, not a newly introduced threshold defect. The retained GLM Graham round-one finding about an exact fabricated tweet adds “posted by a deleted Twitter account”; its cited span says the tweet was deleted, not that the account was deleted. Another selected span ends before the full tweet. The DeepSeek final-round Snopes/Newsweek findings also contain more than some selected quotes alone establish. Audit every returned finding and every component link, including inconvenient/unused evidence, without silently rewriting or dropping them. Invalid selected-passage support is a diagnostic fidelity failure even when a larger context would persuade Jev.

6. **Question guidance preserves the original required assertions.** In `graph.py:294`, `questions_as_guidance` retains generated statements/questions as unverified leads and uses original plus heuristic assertions, matching the statement baseline's obligations. This avoids making a generated reference-date meta-statement an additional factual requirement. It does not certify that the questions preserve scope; inspect the raw plan. `statements_and_questions` has a different obligation set and must not be silently substituted. The indexed analyzer receives actual queries from research-history/round traces. The separate `search_queries` field can still include a selected query not started if a deadline interrupts the selected round; it is not the whole unrestricted inventory. Use the actual traces for executed-search claims and accounting.

7. **The new representation changes evidence exposure as well as formatting.** `indexed_records` takes up to twelve ranked sources, the first 3,500 characters per source, and overlapping 120-word spans. This is neither the old top-two overlap windows nor the earlier global allocator. A ledger result cannot be attributed solely to IDs or questions when those inputs also differ. The 2,399-character retained PolitiFact Graham body fits in full; the 4,697-character Newsweek body is truncated after the relevant Graham discussion and into unrelated material. That particular truncation does not erase its visible no-response qualification. Nevertheless, audit actual per-call visibility before interpreting missing evidence. New visible text does not itself establish efficacy.

8. **Baseline compatibility is promising, not fully established by the present test.** The four ledger/input/output storage fields are annotated with `SkipJsonSchema` to exclude them from baseline request schemas while preserving them in dumps. Compare both complete `EvidenceAssessment` and `BenchmarkAssessment` request schemas with the frozen baseline source/schema, including nested definitions and required fields, before development reuse. Defaults remain standard/statements. Historical reproduction must use the historical archives. I did not independently run the reported full suite or formal archived-schema comparison.

| Control | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Sufficient factual claim | Whole-claim adjudication with exact date/quantity/conditions, direct cited support and documented distinct evidentiary paths; no invented material gap |
| Insufficient embedded-content composite | Evidence that a speaker said X does not establish the additional claim that X is true; leave the unsupported material component unresolved and gate-ineligible |

## Request counts and scope choices

The current `analyze_indexed` accepts one claim and one `IndexedAssessment`. A six-case batch needs an explicitly diagnostic batch envelope, six separate component/record namespaces and six independent calls to the existing compiler. No source or answer may leak between cases. It is a proxy, not the actual single-claim prompt. Freeze batch order; labels remain hidden. No automatic repair or per-case retry follows a batch failure.

| Design | Flash sends | Maximum requested output tokens | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Proposed cheapest proxy | 2 plans + 2 six-case batches = 4 | 2×2,048 + 2×6,144 = 16,384 | Combined semantic/compilation readiness only; no native single-claim response reliability claim |
| **Preferred compromise** | 2 plans + 2 native Graham assessments + 2 five-control batches = **6** | **28,672** | Actual runtime exercised once per model, plus economical semantic controls |
| Entirely native single-claim | 2 plans + 12 assessments = 14 | 77,824 | All six cases individually assessed per model; unnecessary expansion for this initial prescreen |

The preferred six-send phase runs the GLM plan/native anchor/control batch first and inspects them before DeepSeek. A decisive GLM failure stops at three Flash sends or earlier. Plans are source-blind and independent of the later assessment call. The source-blind planner is tested on Graham only; authored control planning cannot be claimed as measured generalization. Input-token estimates must be computed from the actual frozen prompts rather than invented from the output caps. Record actual input/output usage separately.

With at most six findings and one citation per finding per actual anchor, each anchor needs at most one Jev citation batch plus one conditional provenance call: **at most four Jev sends total**, zero retrieval. Enforce the six-finding bound as a prospective diagnostic contract; reject an overlong answer rather than drop citations. Citation review still runs when structural gaps exist; provenance runs only if earlier checks permit. Controls receive full manual semantic/structural audit, but **not a claim of completed automated gate acceptance** under that four-Jev budget. If the objective is instead to run all twelve cases through the actual full Jev gate, the bound is twelve citation batches plus up to eight provenance calls (three positive controls and possibly the anchor per model), **twenty Jev sends**, assuming ineligible controls remain ineligible. That expanded diagnostic is not needed to decide initial readiness.

No concurrent indexed-versus-ledger arm is necessary to estimate the **combined workflow effect** in the frozen baseline comparison. However, this six-send design does not isolate questions, IDs, exposure or ledger effects, and the earlier different S/Q/QL implementation cannot stand in as a causal ablation of this one. Keep identical sources, passages, required assertions, output cap and settings. S→Q probes guidance; Q→QL probes the ledger. All can legitimately abstain.

The executor should choose and freeze one scope before calls, not opportunistically add arms after seeing a result.
